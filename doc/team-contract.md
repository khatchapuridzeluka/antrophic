On Demo Day, anyone answers anything, so backup roles are how we learn each other's parts.

## How we decide
Small things: whoever is doing the work decides. Big things (scope, stack, model choice, pivots): majority vote in the weekly sync or the team chat; on a tie, the spec keeper decides. Every big decision gets a short record in `docs/decisions/` within 48 hours.

## When we work
Weekly sync: Monday, 20:00 Tbilisi time, Google Meet, 30 minutes. We review what merged, what is blocked, and who drives the next slice.
Extra sync: the day before every graded deadline, 20:00, 20 minutes.
Core hours we can all be reached: 18:00–22:00 Tbilisi time, Monday to Friday. We reply in the team chat within 24 hours, and within 4 hours in the two days before a deadline.

## Contribution
Work is visible in commits, pull requests, `docs/delegation-log.md` and our journals in `docs/journal/`. Every pull request is reviewed by someone other than its author. Each member finishes their assigned task by the date agreed in the sync, or says in the team chat at least 24 hours before that they cannot. If someone carries or someone coasts, we say it in week terms, not in Week 15.

## When it breaks
Step 1: name it inside the team within 48 hours, in the sync or the team chat, about the work and not the person.
Step 2: if unresolved in one week, we bring it to the instructor together.
No-show rule: after 2 missed syncs or 2 missed tasks without notice, we raise it with the instructor, and that person's open work is redistributed so deadlines are still met.
